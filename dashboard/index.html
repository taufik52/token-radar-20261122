How implied value is computed

One function scores every row. No protocol name is special-cased; no outside fact is used.

  1. Potential. TVL × a sector multiple (capital 0.34, consumer 0.26, infra 0.18, unknown 0.25), bent by 7-day growth through a tanh curve so an extreme week cannot explode the board, and widened 5% per extra chain (up to 5).
  2. Launch odds. A bounded probability (0.15–0.92) from four snapshot signals: age with no token, 7-day growth, chain breadth, and log TVL. It says the record looks launch-shaped — never that a launch is confirmed or dated.
  3. Implied value. Launch odds × potential, rounded to whole dollars. Rank 1 is the highest.
  4. Confidence & risk. Confidence rises when growth and listing date are reported and falls on thin records or extreme growth. Risk rises for very young, single-chain, or sharply shrinking protocols.

The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: all 40 protocols at or above the $50M floor with no token on record are ranked, plus the 160 largest pre-token protocols below the floor. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside in the “Already trades” tab with their market cap — the market’s own value, never a model’s.

Provenance & limits

This page is fully offline: it reads only the data file written beside it and makes no network request. Farm plans are generic steps derived from each protocol’s category, chains, TVL, and age; they state costs and times, and they never claim a points program, a launch date, or funding the snapshot does not carry. Farming costs gas and can lose money — never farm with funds you cannot afford to lose, and never pay anyone to claim an airdrop.
